Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is the 369 manifestation method?

It is a writing ritual in which you write one chosen statement 3 times in the morning, 6 at midday and 9 at night. This app organizes the practice into a 33-day journey.

Can I write my own desire?

Yes. Choose Wealth, Health, Love, Work, Growth or Peace, or write your desire freely. The app encourages you to write it as already true.

How does the progress spiral work?

Completed days illuminate the Journey spiral. The app also shows your streak, the number of lines written (called “seeds sown”) and days left.

Does the app send reminders?

Yes. You can configure daily reminders for your 3, 6 and 9 writing sessions in Settings.

Is my writing private?

The app does not require an account or cloud storage, and journal writing is kept on your device. You can optionally require your face, fingerprint or passcode to open it.

Is the app free?

The first 7 days are free. After that a single one-time purchase unlocks the app for life — no subscription, no renewal. Everything you have already written stays readable and exportable either way. The purchase is handled by Google Play or the App Store, under their standard refund policies; we never see your payment details.

Can I export my journal?

Yes. Settings includes an option to export your journal as a text file.

Does the 369 method guarantee my desired outcome?

No. The 369 method is a personal intention-writing practice, not a scientifically proven guarantee of external results. It can serve as a structured way to focus, reflect and practice consistency.
